About Travel Nepal

Discover the land of mountains, culture and adventure.

Who We Are

Travel Nepal is a tourism information website created to showcase the natural beauty, cultural heritage and exciting destinations of Nepal.

Our goal is to help visitors discover amazing places, learn about Nepalese traditions and explore the wonderful experiences our country has to offer.

From the beautiful lakes of Pokhara to the historic streets of Kathmandu and the majestic Himalayas, Nepal has something special for every traveler.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To promote Nepal tourism by providing useful information about destinations, culture and travel experiences.

Our Vision

To inspire people around the world to explore Nepal and appreciate its natural and cultural beauty.

Our Values

We believe in promoting responsible tourism, preserving cultural heritage and respecting nature.
